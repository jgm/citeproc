<<===== MODE =====<<

Initialization of a name like "MIsak" abbreviates to the first two
letters (cf. name_LongAbbreviation), lowercasing the second.  With a
Turkish locale, "I" must lowercase to dotless "ı", not "i".

>>===== RESULT =====>>
Mı. Yılmaz
<<===== RESULT =====<<
